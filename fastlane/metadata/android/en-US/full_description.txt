GeoTag Camera is a free, open-source GPS camera with no ads, no tracking and no account. It stamps GPS coordinates, address, date, time and a small map on every photo, writes the location to EXIF, and signs each photo on your phone so later edits can be detected.

Made for field work: site inspections, surveys, construction progress, utility and pole audits, real estate, agriculture, and NGO or student field records.

<b>GPS map camera features</b>
<ul>
<li>Location stamp with live preview: coordinates, address with Plus Code, date and time with GMT offset, accuracy, altitude, bearing and weather</li>
<li>Map thumbnail on the stamp, drawn on the phone from OpenStreetMap data; save whole areas for offline use</li>
<li>Two stamp styles (Bar and Minimal); move the stamp by dragging it</li>
<li>Organization name, logo, a sticky note (for example Pole 12) and a fixed custom text line</li>
<li>Optional hand-drawn signature on the photo</li>
<li>Writes location, altitude, accuracy, direction and time to EXIF for GIS and photo tools</li>
<li>Real camera controls: lens zoom presets, flash, tap-to-focus with exposure, grid, 4:3 / 16:9 / 1:1 / Full, and the phone maker's Night, HDR and Portrait modes where offered</li>
<li>Saves to your gallery in Pictures/GeoTagCamera; the in-app gallery groups photos by day, with share, delete, open in maps and copy coordinates</li>
<li>Projects: shoot into a project, filter and move photos</li>
<li>Export photo locations as CSV, KML (Google Earth) or GeoJSON (QGIS, ArcGIS)</li>
<li>PDF site report with an overview map and every photo re-verified</li>
<li>Simple and Professional setups, chosen on first launch</li>
</ul>

<b>Tamper-evident photos</b>
Each photo is hashed with SHA-256 and signed with a key kept in the Android Keystore on your phone. The proof travels inside the file, so any copy of the app can verify any photo offline. "Verified" means the image, latitude, longitude and capture time have not changed since signing. It does not prove who took the photo or that the phone's GPS and clock were right. Re-saving or compressing a photo breaks verification.

<b>Privacy</b>
No ads, analytics or trackers, and nothing is sent to the developer. Location is used only while the camera is open. Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap (no account or key). The weather chip is off by default; when on, it sends your position rounded to about 1 km to Open-Meteo. Addresses come from the phone's built-in Android geocoder, which on most phones is run by Google and uses the network. App data is excluded from cloud backup and can be wiped from the About screen. No Google Play services are needed.

<b>Common questions</b>
<b>Is GeoTag Camera free?</b> Yes. It is free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later), with no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.
<b>Does it work offline?</b> Yes. Taking, stamping, signing and verifying photos work without signal. Offline, the stamp uses the nearest cached address, and the Plus Code is always calculated on the phone.
<b>Does it need Google Play services?</b> No. Location comes from Android's own location services.
<b>Can I get the photo locations out?</b> Yes, as CSV, KML, GeoJSON or a PDF report.
